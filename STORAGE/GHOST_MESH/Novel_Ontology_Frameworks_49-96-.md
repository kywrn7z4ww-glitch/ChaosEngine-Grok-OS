# 48 Novel Ontology Frameworks (49–96)

The following frameworks continue the series begun in frameworks 1–48. Each synthesizes at least three fundamental dimensions—epistemic, semantic, thermodynamic, holographic, fractal, autopoietic, computational, quantum, participatory, Gödelian, consciousness, logical, informational, causal, or geometric—into a coherent and unprecedented ontological model. Every framework is distinct from all prior entries and introduces novel combinations, mechanisms, and equations.

### 49. **Torsional‑Epistemic‑Semantic Ontology**

*Key equation:* \( T^{\lambda}_{\ \mu\nu}^{(\text{epistemic})} = \Gamma^{\lambda}_{\mu\nu} - \Gamma^{\lambda}_{\nu\mu} \neq 0 \), with the Cartan equation \( dS_{\text{semantic}} = T^{(\text{epistemic})} \wedge e_{\text{meaning}} \)
*Implication:* The gap between experience and language is geometrically fundamental—not a defect but a torsional source.
