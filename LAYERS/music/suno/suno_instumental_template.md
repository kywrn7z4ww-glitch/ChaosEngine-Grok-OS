# Suno Instrumental Best Practices (2026)

**Why Suno has a vocal bias:**
- Trained mostly on vocal-heavy music
- Optimized to create "complete songs" (vocals = more engaging)
- Strong default assumption that music includes singing

**How to beat the bias:**
- The `[Instrumental]` + anti-vocal line at the top is the strongest defense
- Use dense technical jargon (this pushes Suno into production mode)
- Avoid song structure words (Verse, Chorus, Bridge) unless you're actually writing lyrics
- Keep prompts focused — overly long or contradictory prompts reduce effectiveness

**Pro Tips:**
- Technical language (especially microtonal theory and production terms) helps Suno understand you want a produced track, not a song
- Repeating the anti-vocal instruction 2–3 times in different parts of the prompt increases success rate
- Suno v4.5/v5 is significantly better than older versions, but the bias still exists and requires deliberate prompting
